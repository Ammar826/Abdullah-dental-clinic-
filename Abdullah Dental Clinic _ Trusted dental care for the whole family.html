Questions we’re often asked

Will the treatment hurt?

We aim to keep every visit comfortable. We explain each step before we start, and you can ask us to pause at any time.

How often should I get a check-up?

Most people benefit from a check-up about every six months, but we’ll advise what’s right for you.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We welcome children and take the time to make the first visit friendly.

How will I know the cost?

We explain your options and prices before any treatment begins, so there are no surprises.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call us as soon as you can and we’ll do our best to see you the same day.
